# Offline AI for iPhone

Most AI apps send every message to a data center, so they stop the moment the connection does. Dotislash is different: its AI models run on the iPhone itself. Once a model is downloaded, it answers with no Wi-Fi and no signal.

<div class="box">
<p><strong>Try it yourself:</strong> turn on Airplane Mode and ask anything. If it answers, nothing was sent.</p>
</div>

## What works offline

- **Chat and writing.** Ask questions, draft emails and messages, rewrite text, brainstorm.
- **Translation.** Translate phrases and documents between languages, on a flight or abroad without data.
- **Your files.** Read PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and EPUB files and photos, and ask about them, summarize them or rewrite them.
- **Slides and documents.** Make PowerPoint decks and Word documents you can edit and share.
- **First aid.** Step-by-step cards for CPR, choking, bleeding and burns open at once. General first-aid information, not medical advice; in an emergency, call emergency services first.
- **Your data.** Saved chats, memory and the app lock all live on the iPhone.

## What needs a connection

Only two things go online, and only when you use them: downloading a model, once, and searching the web for today's facts. Web search can be switched off in Settings, and every search shows in the reply. With it off, nothing leaves the iPhone at all.

## Where it helps

- On a plane, with no Wi-Fi or with Wi-Fi you don't want to pay for.
- Abroad, without roaming data.
- In a blackout, a storm or a place with no signal.
- In a secure building where devices stay offline.

## Which iPhones

Dotislash picks a model your iPhone has the memory for. A small model runs on iPhone 11 and older, a light one from 4 GB of memory, the 35B Pro model from 8 GB, and the 180B Max model on iPhones with 12 GB. See [the models](/models/).
