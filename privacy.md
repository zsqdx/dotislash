# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

Dotislash, by Suqin Zhang, is an AI assistant that runs on your iPhone. This page says what that means for your data.

<div class="box">
<p><strong>We collect no data through the app.</strong> There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We only receive what you choose to email us.</p>
<p><strong>The AI runs on your iPhone.</strong> Your chats, the files and photos you attach, what it remembers about you and the documents it makes are stored only on your device.</p>
<p><strong>Some features go online, straight from your iPhone</strong> to the service that answers them, never through us. The table below lists each one, and each can be switched off.</p>
</div>

## What stays on your iPhone

Your conversations and their attachments, the memory you build up, the slides and documents you make, your settings, the models you download and a diagnostic log. The models process everything on the device. Speech is turned into text on the device. A content filter that checks messages also runs on the device and sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Like the rest of your iPhone, it may be included in device backups that you make and control, except the models, which are left out.

The diagnostic log records which model was loaded, how fast it ran and which features were used, to help solve problems. It doesn't contain your conversations, and it leaves your iPhone only if you share it (Settings → Diagnostics → Share the log).

## When the app goes online

| What you do | Who receives what |
|---|---|
| Download a model | Hugging Face (huggingface.co), or one of its mirrors (hf-mirror.com, modelscope.cn) when that is faster, receives the request for the file. |
| Ask something that needs current information, with web search on | The words of your question, and for a follow-up the question before it, go to Bing and DuckDuckGo, or to Wikipedia if both fail. Your iPhone then opens the top results' websites to read them. |
| Ask about the weather, the news or a share price | Weather comes from Open-Meteo and wttr.in, headlines from Google News, prices from Yahoo Finance. They receive the place, topic or ticker you asked about. |
| Paste a link | Your iPhone opens that page from its website. |
| Ask about places near you or how to get somewhere, with location on | Your position goes to Apple Maps to find places and routes, and to the weather services for a local forecast. Your town's name may be added to a web search. |
| Ask for a reminder or a calendar event | Nothing goes online. The app adds it to, or reads it from, Reminders or Calendar on your iPhone, with your permission. |
| Tap Report under a response | An email to us, containing that response, which you see and send yourself. |
| Buy Dotislash Pro or Max, or restore a purchase | Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive no name, email or card details from it. The app asks Apple, on your iPhone, which plan is active, and counts the free replies and files left on your iPhone. |

Each of these services receives the request directly from your iPhone, including your IP address, as with any visit to a website, and its own privacy policy applies. We do not receive any of these requests or their results.

Every search and page the assistant used is shown in its reply.

## Your choices

- **Web search:** turn off Settings → Search the web when needed. The assistant then answers only from what it knows.
- **Location:** turn off Settings → Use my location, or change the permission in the iPhone's Settings.
- **Memory:** Settings → Memory shows what it remembers. Delete any line, forget everything, or turn memory off.
- **Reminders, Calendar, microphone, speech:** each asks for permission the first time, and can be changed in the iPhone's Settings.
- **Delete data:** delete a chat from the drawer, a model from Models, or the app to remove everything it stored.

## If you contact us

If you email us, to ask for help or to report a response, we receive your email address and what you sent. We use it only to reply to you and to improve Dotislash. We don't share it, and we delete it once it's no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the matter is closed. To have it deleted sooner, ask.

## Children

Dotislash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
