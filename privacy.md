# Dotislash Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 4, 2026*

Dotislash, by Suqin Zhang, runs AI language models on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## The short version

- **We collect no data through the app.** Dotislash has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We only receive what you choose to email us.
- **Your chats stay on your iPhone unless you act.** Conversations, and the files and photos you attach to them, are stored only in Dotislash's storage on your device, and the models process them there. They leave your iPhone only as described below: through the optional web tools if you turn them on, and in a report if you send one.
- **Some features connect to other services, at your request:** model downloads come from Hugging Face, and the optional Wikipedia and web-page tools contact Wikipedia and the websites a model opens. Details are below.

## Data on your iPhone

Dotislash stores your conversations, their attachments, the models you download, your settings, the state of any download and a diagnostic log in its own storage on your iPhone. The models process everything on the device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Like the rest of your iPhone, it may be included in device backups that you make and control, except the models, which are left out of backups.

The diagnostic log records which model was loaded, how fast it ran, and the names of tools used and of any content filter category that applied, to help solve problems. It doesn't contain your conversations. It leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share it (Settings → Diagnostics → Share the log).

The content filter runs entirely on your iPhone. It sends nothing anywhere.

## Connections Dotislash makes

**Model downloads.** When you download a model, Dotislash fetches its files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), run by Hugging Face, Inc. Like any website, Hugging Face receives standard request information, such as your IP address and the file requested. See the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

**Wikipedia and web pages (optional, off by default).** If you turn on this tool, which Dotislash asks you to confirm, a model can use it during a reply:

- to search Wikipedia, which sends the search terms to the Wikimedia Foundation;
- to open web pages, which are fetched directly from their websites.

The model writes these searches and addresses itself, from your conversation, so they can include what you typed or attached. Each site receives the request, including your IP address and that it comes from Dotislash, as with any visit. Dotislash refuses private and local network addresses. See the [Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). Every search and page the model uses is shown in the reply. Those sites are independent of us, and their own policies apply.

**Python (optional, off by default).** The Python tool runs code inside a sandbox on your iPhone. The sandbox has no internet access and no access to your files.

Dotislash makes no other connections. We do not receive any of these requests or their results.

## If you contact us

If you email us, for example to ask for help or to report a response with Report, we receive what you send: your email address and your message. A report contains the response, and can also contain the model's reasoning and tool results, such as web text, Python code and its output. You see it all before you send it. We use it only to reply to you and to improve Dotislash's safety. We don't share it, and we delete it once it's no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the matter is closed.

## Your choices

- **Turn off a tool:** switch off Wikipedia and web pages or Python in Settings → Tools at any time. A model can then no longer use it. Requests already made can't be recalled, and the sites' own policies apply to them.
- **Delete data on your iPhone:** delete a chat from the list of chats, delete a model from the Models tab, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
- **Delete what you sent us:** email zsqdx2015@gmail.com and ask us to delete your messages. We'll confirm when it's done.

## Children

Dotislash is not directed at children. Its age rating is shown on its App Store page.
