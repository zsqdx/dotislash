# Models

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

Dotislash runs its AI models on the iPhone itself. You pick a size on the Models page; the app offers the ones your iPhone has the memory for, and downloads no others.

## Mini

**1.3 GB download · for iPhone 11 and older**

Made for older iPhones, whose graphics chip the larger models can't use well. It chats and answers; it has no tools.

## Lite

**1.2 GB download · for an iPhone with 4 GB of memory or more · free without limits**

Small and quick to download: a good way to try the app, and the one for iPhones with less memory.

## Standard

**2.5 GB download · for an iPhone with 8 GB of memory or more · 20 replies a day free, no limit with Dotislash Pro**

The fast default. It reads photos and looks things up on the web.

## Pro

**14.6 GB download · for an iPhone with 8 GB of memory or more · 5 replies a day free, no limit with Dotislash Pro**

Stronger answers, a little slower. Asked to research something, it decides what to look up and reads the pages.

## Max

**66.4 GB download · for an iPhone with 12 GB of memory · 3 replies a day free, no limit with Dotislash Max**

For deep research. Much stronger, much slower, and a very large download.

## Good to know

Reading photos needs a vision file of 0.4 to 0.9 GB, which downloads with the model. The app shows the two sizes together before you download.

The models are open-weight models, used under their publishers' licenses. Each model's license is shown beside it on the Models page of the app.

Models can make mistakes, and they can write things you may not want to read.
