# An AI agent that runs on your iPhone

Most AI agents think in a data center. Dotislash plans every step on the iPhone itself: what to look up, which pages to open, when it has enough to answer, and what to make.

## What the agent does

- **Decides what a request needs.** It answers at once when it knows, and goes to the web by itself when the answer needs today's facts.
- **Searches and reads.** It searches the web from your iPhone, opens the pages that matter and answers with its sources.
- **Shows every step.** Each search and each page it read appears in the reply.
- **Checks its figures.** A number it couldn't find in what it read is marked, so you know which ones to verify.
- **Works numbers out.** It calculates instead of guessing.
- **Makes things.** PowerPoint decks, Word documents and small games, ready to edit and share.
- **Runs on a schedule.** Ask it for the news or the weather every morning.

## Big models, on a phone

- **Pro:** a 35B model, fully offline, on iPhones with 8 GB of memory or more.
- **Max:** a 180B model on iPhones with 12 GB of memory.
- **Dig deeper:** research that takes its time, on the bigger model.

## Why on the iPhone

Your question, the files you attach and the agent's reasoning stay on the device. Only the searches go out, and one switch turns them off. And when there is no connection at all, it still answers from what it knows.
