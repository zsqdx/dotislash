An agent that lives on your iPhone

Other agents think in a data center. This one plans every step on your phone: what to look up, which pages to open, and when it has enough to answer.

It decides what to do

Ask anything, in any words. It answers at once when it knows, and goes to the web by itself when the answer needs today's facts.

You watch every step

Each search and each page it reads appears as it happens, and the answer names its sources.

It checks its own figures

A number it couldn't find in what it read is marked, so you know which ones to verify.

Your question stays yours
