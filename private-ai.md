# Private AI for confidential documents

A client's contract, a medical report, a payslip, a file that may not leave the company: the things people hold back from a cloud AI. Dotislash reads them on the iPhone, and nothing is uploaded.

<div class="box">
<p><strong>No account, no server, no analytics, no ads.</strong> The AI model runs on your iPhone, so your documents and your questions about them stay there.</p>
</div>

## What you can do with a document

- **Summarize** a contract, a report or a long PDF in plain language.
- **Flag the risks** in an agreement: auto-renewals, uncapped liability, one-sided terms.
- **Ask questions** about a specific clause, figure or date.
- **Rewrite** a section: shorter, more formal, or in another language.
- **Turn it into slides** or a Word document you can edit and share.

It reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and EPUB files and photos.

## How it stays private

- **On the iPhone.** The model processes everything on the device. There is no server of ours to send it to.
- **Locked.** Turn on Face ID or Touch ID for the app, and its contents are hidden in the app switcher.
- **Erasable.** One tap erases every chat, file and memory.
- **Verifiable.** Turn on Airplane Mode and it still works, which shows nothing is being sent.
- **Optional web.** Web search only runs when a question needs today's facts, every search is shown, and one switch turns it off.

Read the [privacy policy](/privacy.html) for exactly what goes online, when, and to whom.

## Who it's for

- Lawyers, accountants, consultants and people in finance and health care, whose clients' documents can't go to a cloud AI.
- Anyone with a private paper: a lease, a medical report, a letter from the bank.
- Employees whose company restricts outside AI because data may not leave.
