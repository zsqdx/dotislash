## Getting started

<details><summary>How do I start?</summary>
<p>Open the app and tap <strong>Download and start</strong>. The AI model downloads once, about 2.5 GB on most iPhones, and after that the app works without a connection. Then type a message, tap a suggestion, or tap the waveform to talk.</p></details>

<details><summary>Which model should I choose?</summary>
<p>The welcome page picks one for your iPhone: Standard where it has 8 GB of memory or more, Lite otherwise. Both read photos and look things up on the web. The Models page lists every size, from Lite to Max, and says so if your iPhone doesn't have the memory for one. See <a href="/models/">Models</a> for sizes and requirements.</p></details>

<details><summary>A download is taking a long time.</summary>
<p>The page shows the speed while it downloads. Keep the app open on Wi-Fi for the fastest download; it carries on in the background if you leave. If it says "Waiting for Wi-Fi…", join a Wi-Fi network, or turn on <strong>Settings → Downloads → Use cellular data</strong> and download again. A download that stops picks up where it left off when it can.</p></details>

<details><summary>How do I change the language?</summary>
<p><strong>Settings → Language</strong>: English, 简体中文 or 繁體中文. The assistant itself answers in the language you write in.</p></details>

<details><summary>Where are my chats, and the other pages?</summary>
<p>Swipe right anywhere, or tap the button at the top left. The drawer has New chat, Documents, Models and Settings, and your saved chats under them. Touch and hold a chat to rename or delete it.</p></details>

## What it can do

<details><summary>What is the agent, and why does a reply show steps?</summary>
<p>The assistant decides on your iPhone what a message needs. When the answer depends on today's facts, such as prices, news or opening hours, it looks them up itself: you see each search and each page it reads as a step, then the answer with its sources. Ask it to research or compare something and it works through several steps before it answers. Numbers that weren't in what it read are underlined.</p></details>

<details><summary>How does web search work?</summary>
<p>You don't have to ask for it or word a message in any special way: the assistant searches when a question needs current information, and shows which pages it read. Paste a link into a message and it reads that page. Switch it off in <strong>Settings → Search the web when needed</strong>, and nothing leaves your iPhone.</p></details>

<details><summary>How do I make slides or a document?</summary>
<p>Say it: "make a slide deck about our Q3 results" or "write a one-page project update as a Word document". Tap the file in the reply to edit it, change its theme, play it as a slideshow, or share it as PowerPoint or PDF. Then just say what to change: "make slide 2 shorter", "make it dark", "add a slide about pricing".</p></details>

<details><summary>Can it use my own PowerPoint template?</summary>
<p>Yes. Attach your PowerPoint or Keynote file and say "use this template". The deck keeps the template's backgrounds, logo and type. In the slide editor you can also pick a template under Theme.</p></details>

<details><summary>Which files can it read?</summary>
<p>Tap + beside the message box to add a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Pages, Numbers, Keynote or EPUB file, or a photo, then ask about it. Reading photos uses a vision file, which downloads with the model.</p></details>

<details><summary>How do I talk to it?</summary>
<p>Tap the waveform at the right of the message box and speak. It answers out loud; tap the circle to interrupt. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone.</p></details>

<details><summary>What does it remember?</summary>
<p>Tell it what to keep in mind ("remember that I'm vegetarian") and it uses that in later chats. <strong>Settings → Memory</strong> shows what it knows and lets you delete any of it, or turn memory off.</p></details>

<details><summary>Why does it ask for my location?</summary>
<p>For questions like "coffee near me", "how long to the airport" or "will it rain today". It is used only for questions that need it, and you can switch it off in <strong>Settings → Use my location</strong>.</p></details>

## Problems

<details><summary>The app closed while a model was loading or replying.</summary>
<p>iOS closes apps that use more memory than it allows. Close other apps, or restart your iPhone, and try again. A smaller model uses less memory.</p></details>

<details><summary>Replies are slow.</summary>
<p>Speed depends on the model and your iPhone, and a warm phone is slower. For a quick check, load a model and open <strong>Settings → Diagnostics → Measure speed</strong>.</p></details>

<details><summary>Why did the app refuse my message, or replace an answer?</summary>
<p>A filter on your iPhone stops sexual content involving minors, explicit sexual content and instructions that could seriously hurt people, and answers messages about self-harm with crisis-line information. It matches patterns, so it can be wrong. Nothing about it leaves your iPhone. If it got something wrong, tell us.</p></details>

<details><summary>How do I report a wrong or harmful response?</summary>
<p>Tap <strong>Report</strong> under the response. This opens an email to us with the response, which you see before you send it.</p></details>

<details><summary>How do I delete my data?</summary>
<p>Touch and hold a chat in the drawer to delete it. Swipe left on a model in Models to delete it. <strong>Settings → Memory</strong> clears what it remembers. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. To have an email you sent us deleted, write to the address above.</p></details>
